Who can open it: SWRF volunteers with an email that ends in @stormywx.org, and partners SWRF has added.

If Google asks you to log in: use your @stormywx.org email (partners: use the email SWRF has for you).

Not working?

  • Wrong account, or "unable to open the file"? Your phone or computer may be logged in to more than one Google account. Open this page in a private (incognito) window, tap the button again, and log in with your @stormywx.org email.
  • Opened inside your email app? Tap the menu and choose "Open in browser" (Safari or Chrome), then tap the button.
  • Still stuck? Call or text Rob at 864-616-6579.

Need help after the flood? This page is not for you. Please use our flood help request form. If you are in danger, call 911.
